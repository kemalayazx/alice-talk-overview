## Interpretation

Boxes, couriers, shelves and keys are educational metaphors. No real addresses, routing labels, cryptographic key material or messages are shown. A network participant may observe its immediate neighbors; the proposed multi-stop scene is not a claim of complete anonymity or a claim that Alice Talk uses Tor. The present prototype remains relay-assisted.

Current code-enforced messenger choices and future general agent authorization are separate. The written project brief provides the full limitations and development-evidence scope.

## Production method

Original vector-style shapes were drawn and animated programmatically. Existing local system voices (Samantha for English, Yelda for Turkish) synthesized the reviewed text. Caption boundaries follow separately generated utterances. The quiet musical pad was synthesized originally without an external recording. No voice imitation of a real individual is intended.

Representative frames from all eleven scenes were inspected for composition and legibility. Technical media checks do not replace independent security review or full independent listening verification.
